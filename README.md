# Spooky to Snowy

Family days out in and around Milton Keynes, October to December 2026: Halloween walks and shows, Santa steam trains, pantomime, zoo light trails, nature walks and more.

Live page: https://gangulydrkuntal.github.io/spooky-to-snowy/

- Browse by date, by type or on a calendar, and filter by month, date range, price and age group
- Save favourites to "My plan" (stored in your browser)
- Prices, booking links and car or train directions from Central Milton Keynes for every event
- Add events or booking reminders to Google or Outlook Calendar

Prices and times can change, so always confirm on the organiser's booking page before you pay.
